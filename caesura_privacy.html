Caesura Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Caesura is a menu bar app for macOS that reminds you to take short eye breaks and longer rest breaks. It has no accounts and keeps your data on your Mac. Here is exactly what it uses and why.

What the App Uses on Your Mac

← Camera (optional, off by default) — only if you turn on eye tracking. Each frame is analysed on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework to tell whether you are facing the screen and whether your eyes are open. Frames are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere.
← Whether your microphone or camera is busy — so a break waits while you are in a call. Caesura asks macOS which apps are using an audio input and whether a camera is running. It never captures, listens to or records audio or video.
← Fullscreen video — the position of on-screen windows and whether an app is keeping the display awake, so a break does not interrupt a video or presentation. Window contents are never read.
← Idle time — how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input, so idle time does not count as work. What you type or click is never read.

What Stays on Your Device

✓ Break statistics (completed and skipped breaks per day) are stored locally in the app's preferences for 90 days, including the confirmation text you type to skip a rest break. That text never leaves your Mac
✓ Your settings and break messages are stored locally in the app's preferences
✓ All camera processing for eye tracking happens on your Mac
✓ Notifications, banners and break screens are generated locally

Anonymous Usage Analytics (Opt-In)

Analytics are off unless you turn them on. While they are off, Caesura sends nothing to TelemetryDeck, not even an app-start signal. If you turn on "Share Anonymous Analytics" in Settings or during onboarding, Caesura sends usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. You can turn this off at any time in Settings; turning it off sends one last signal recording that, and nothing after it.

Signals describe how the app is used, for example: the app started or quit, a break started, finished, was skipped or snoozed, an onboarding page or settings tab was opened, a menu item was used, eye tracking was turned on or off, and whether camera access was granted. Settings changes are sent as coarse ranges (for example "16–25 minutes"), not exact values. Session length and days since install are also sent as ranges.

The TelemetryDeck SDK adds technical details such as app version, macOS version, Mac model, screen size, language, region and time zone. Instead of a personal identifier it sends a salted, hashed random identifier, so signals cannot be linked to you.

Signals never include camera images, face data, audio, window contents, keystrokes, your name or email address.

What We Don't Do

✗ No accounts, no sign-ups, no cloud sync
✗ No recording or storing of camera images or audio
✗ No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites
✗ No data sold or shared with anyone; the only network service the app uses is TelemetryDeck, and only when you opt in

Your Choices

✓ Turn analytics on or off in Caesura's Settings
✓ Turn eye tracking off in Caesura's Settings, or revoke camera access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera
✓ Turn off holding breaks during calls from the menu bar
✓ Deleting the app and its preferences removes your statistics and settings

Children's Privacy

Caesura does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If what Caesura uses or sends changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
